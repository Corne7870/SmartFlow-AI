How much does website design cost in South Africa?
Our website design packages start from R5,500 (approximately $300 USD). Every project is custom-quoted based on your specific requirements, number of pages, and features needed. We offer transparent pricing with no hidden fees. Get a free quote →
What is an AI voice receptionist and how does it work?
An AI voice receptionist is an intelligent virtual assistant that handles your business calls 24/7 using advanced natural language processing. It can answer questions, book appointments, qualify leads, and transfer urgent calls to your team — all with a natural, human-like voice. It's like having a full-time receptionist that never takes a day off. Learn more →
Do you serve clients outside of South Africa?
Yes! While we're proudly based in South Africa with a strong focus on the Eastern Cape and nationwide clients, we serve businesses internationally. Our digital services — website design and AI receptionist setup — can be delivered remotely to clients anywhere in the world.
How long does it take to build a business website?
Most small business websites are completed within 2-4 weeks depending on complexity. Simple business websites can be ready in as little as 7 days, while more complex projects with custom features may take 4-6 weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline during your free consultation.
Can the AI receptionist integrate with my existing systems?
Absolutely. Our AI voice receptionist integrates with popular calendar systems (Google Calendar, Calendly), CRM platforms, and phone systems. We handle the entire setup and configuration process for you — no technical knowledge required on your end.
Do you offer ongoing website maintenance and support?
Yes, all our website packages include 30 days of free support after launch. We also offer affordable monthly maintenance plans that include content updates, security monitoring, backups, performance optimization, and priority support.

Every website we build is mobile-first responsive and fully SEO-optimized.
